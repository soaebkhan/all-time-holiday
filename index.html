Tour Itinerary Breakdown

A carefully designed day-wise journey through Kashmir's best destinations.

DAY 01 – Arrival in Srinagar + Local Sightseeing

Day 1
  • Arrival at Srinagar Airport & meet/greet by representative
  • Transfer to hotel, check-in & freshen up
  • Proceed for Srinagar local sightseeing: Mughal Gardens, Nishat Garden, Chashme Shahi, Botanical Garden & Hazratbal Shrine
  • Explore Srinagar local market
  • Evening Shikara Ride on Dal Lake
  • Return to hotel for Dinner & Overnight Stay
🏨 Overnight Stay: Srinagar Hotel

DAY 02 – Srinagar → Gulmarg → Srinagar

Day 2
  • Breakfast at the hotel
  • Full-day excursion to Gulmarg through scenic Kashmir Valley
  • Explore Gulmarg surroundings and visit Gulmarg Gondola area
  • Gondola Ride can be taken at additional cost (subject to availability)
  • Enjoy leisure time and photography
  • Evening return to Srinagar for Dinner & Overnight Stay
🏨 Overnight Stay: Srinagar Hotel

DAY 03 – Srinagar → Sonamarg → Houseboat

Day 3
  • Breakfast at hotel & check-out
  • Full-day excursion to Sonamarg (Golden Meadow)
  • Explore Sonamarg and visit Thajiwas Glacier area (by local transport / pony subject to weather)
  • Enjoy leisure time in Sonamarg
  • Return to Srinagar & check-in to traditional Kashmiri Houseboat
  • Dinner & Overnight stay in Houseboat
🏨 Overnight Stay: Houseboat, Srinagar

DAY 04 – Houseboat → Pahalgam

Day 4
  • Breakfast at houseboat & check-out
  • Proceed towards Pahalgam with en-route apple orchards and mountain views
  • Visit Pahalgam & explore scenic surroundings
  • Optional sightseeing to Betaab Valley, Aru Valley & Chandanwari (subject to local vehicles)
  • Check-in at hotel, Dinner & Overnight stay in Pahalgam
🏨 Overnight Stay: Pahalgam Hotel

DAY 05 – Pahalgam → Srinagar Departure

Day 5
  • Breakfast at hotel & check-out
  • Drive towards Srinagar
  • Free time for shopping / local sightseeing (depending on flight time)
  • Transfer to Srinagar Airport for departure

5 Days Sample Tour Itinerary

A carefully crafted plan to help you experience Kashmir completely.

Day 1

Srinagar Arrival & Houseboat Stay

Pickup from Airport, transfer to Luxury Houseboat. Enjoy 1-Hour Evening Shikara Ride on Dal Lake.

Day 2

Srinagar Local Sightseeing

Visit Mughal Gardens, Nishat Bagh, Shalimar Bagh, Pari Mahal & Shankaracharya Temple.

Day 3

Srinagar to Gulmarg Day Trip

Explore snow peaks, world's highest Gondola Cable Car Ride, and Meadow of Flowers.

Day 4

Pahalgam (Valley of Shepherds)

Visit Betaab Valley, Aru Valley, and Chandanwari along the scenic Lidder River.

Day 5

Departure from Srinagar

Drop at Srinagar Airport with sweet memories of Kashmir.
